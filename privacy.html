Python from zero Terms

Privacy policy

What the course keeps about you, why, who else sees it, and how to remove it.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Who runs the course

Python from zero is run by Duke Azeta, in Nigeria. Questions about your data: azetaduke@gmail.com.

What we keep

Lessons and exercises work without an account. When you sign in with Google, the course keeps:

  • From your Google account: your name, email address, profile picture link, and Google's ID number for your account. We never see your Google password.
  • Your learning: which lessons you've started and finished, how many tries each exercise took and whether it passed, your quiz answers, and recent mistakes (error messages and which tests failed). The tutor uses these to aim reviews and practice at what you find hard.
  • Tutor chats: the questions you ask the tutor and its replies, up to the last 40 on each lesson.
  • Other things you choose: your avatar, and any feedback you send about a lesson.
  • Running the service: when you were last active, how many tutor requests you made each day (for the daily limit), and your sign-in session.

Exercises run Python inside your own browser. When you run or check one, the result is sent to the course so it can save your progress. Your browser may also keep your exercise code on your device, so it's still there when you come back.

The AI tutor and Google Gemini

The tutor is Google's Gemini. When you use it, the course sends Google your question, and for "Explain my mistake" your code, its output and the error. It also sends the lesson text and your recent mistakes, so the answer fits where you are. Google handles this under its Gemini API terms. Depending on the type of Gemini account the course uses, Google may use this content to improve its products. Don't put personal information in your questions or code.

Other services your browser talks to

  • Google: for signing in, for your profile picture, and for the fonts on these pages.
  • jsDelivr: your browser downloads Pyodide, the Python that runs the exercises, from the jsDelivr network.
  • YouTube: lessons show small video pictures (thumbnails) from YouTube. A video only loads when you press play. It plays through youtube-nocookie.com, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies to what you watch.
  • X: only if you point at the X icon on the home page, your browser loads the profile pictures for its preview from X.

These services see your IP address when your browser connects to them, as with any website.

Cookies

The course sets two cookies, both needed for signing in: one keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and one lasts 10 minutes while a sign-in is happening. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and nothing tracks you across other websites.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share your progress with anyone except the services above, which are needed to run the course.

How long we keep it, and removing it

  • Download it: "Download my progress" in the account menu gives you a copy of your saved learning data.
  • Delete it: "Delete my account" in the account menu removes your account, progress, mistakes and chats straight away. Feedback you sent is kept without your name. Copies in the course's daily backups are gone within 14 days.
  • Otherwise we keep your data while your account exists.

You can also ask us to correct or delete anything at azetaduke@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, data protection law may give you further rights, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Age

The course is for people aged 13 or over. If you're younger than the age of digital consent where you live, ask a parent or guardian first.

Keeping it safe

The course uses HTTPS. Only a scrambled version (a hash) of each sign-in cookie is stored, and only the person running the course can reach the database.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. For big changes, we'll tell signed-in learners on the course.
